The Cyber Cougars are Mid-State Technical College's IT club. The look borrows straight from the college — Mid-State maroon, gold and rich black — and adds a charcoal "tech" ground for the club's own pieces.

## Voice

- Plain, direct, a little warm. Write like a student telling another student what's up.
- Sentence case everywhere except eyebrows, the club name on mastheads/slides, and meta bars, which are UPPERCASE.
- Say who does what: "The students run the Cyber Cougars. The advisor advises."
- Bold the first sentence of a paragraph when it carries the point.
- Dates: `Thursday 24 September · 12:00 PM`. Use `·` between facts, an em dash before a detail ("Tech Con 2.1 — volunteers wanted").
- No emoji.

## Colour

- `maroon` is the brand. Primary buttons, date pills, callout and panel rules, the badge ring.
- `gold` is the spark — one gold thing per view: "Open to all students", a slide subtitle. Never gold text on a light ground.
- Pages sit on `surface` with `surface-raised` panels; text is `text`, secondary copy `text-muted`.
- Links, eyebrows and secondary buttons use `brand-text`: maroon in light theme, gold in dark (maroon on charcoal fails contrast).
- Mastheads and slides are always `masthead` charcoal with `text-on-dark` and a gold accent, in either theme.
- Focus: 2px solid `focus` ring, 2px offset.

## Type

- One family: Montserrat (Google Fonts), standing in for the college's Gotham. Gotham is listed second in the stack for machines that have it.
- `display` 800 UPPERCASE for the club name; `subtitle` gold UPPERCASE tracked wide under it.
- `h1` for page sections, `h2` for card titles, `body` for copy, `eyebrow` above blocks, `small` for fine print and pills.

## Shape and space

- Tight, squared corners: `radius-sm` on buttons and rows, `radius-md` on panels, `radius-pill` only on date pills.
- A 3px maroon rule on the left edge marks the club's content blocks (callouts, panels, event rows) — it's the club page's signature.
- Spacing runs `space-1`–`space-8`; panels pad `space-4`/`space-5`.
- No shadows. Separation is `divider` hairlines and the maroon rule.

## Logos and imagery

- The club badge (`Logos/cyber-cougars-badge.png`) is the mark. Centre it on mastheads, left of the name on slides with a maroon vertical rule between.
- Use the Mid-State logo only on light grounds; it's single-ink maroon.
- The slanted three-bar pattern (from the college logo and Teams backgrounds) is the one decorative motif.
- For Teams meetings use `Backgrounds/Mid-State Teams Background-Dark.jpg`.
